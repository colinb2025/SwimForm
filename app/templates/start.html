Two clips, two angles

One from the side and one head-on. Each angle sees what the other can't.

A few clean strokes is enough. Swim easy and the way you normally do: no fins, paddles or pull buoy.

{{ mini_diagram("side") }}

The side clip

Someone on the deck points the phone straight across your lane and walks alongside as you swim a 50, down and back.

Your whole body in shot, head to feet. No zooming.

{{ mini_diagram("head_on") }}

The head-on clip

From the end wall, in the middle of your lane. Hold the phone high, look down the lane, and keep it still as you swim toward it.

Stop filming before you reach the wall. Looking down sees through the surface; a low camera mostly sees glare.

Set up the phone

Turn it sideways. Film in 1080p at 60 frames a second if your phone offers it, and not in slow motion.

Upload the original video, not a screen recording or a copy sent through a chat app. Underwater clips are optional. Above-water clips are all you need.

You can find all of this again under Filming guide.
